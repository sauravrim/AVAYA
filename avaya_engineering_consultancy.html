How We Work

Streamlined Engineering Workflow

A transparent, four-stage delivery methodology from initial field measurement to certified handover.

STEP 01

Site Visit & Requirement Review

On-site inspection, land boundary verification, soil/topography assessment, and client scope alignment.

STEP 02

Technical Drafting & Assessment

CAD architectural drafting, structural load calculations, or detailed property market & cadastral valuation analysis.

STEP 03

NEC & Regulatory Verification

Quality audit against Nepal National Building Code (NBC), municipal bylaws, and NEC engineer certification.

STEP 04

Certified Handover & Supervision

Delivery of approved Naksa Pass documents or bank valuation reports, followed by scheduled site supervision.
